# Product

## What Oreum is

Oreum is a data-driven market-intelligence tool for investors who want to
understand what is happening in the market without constantly researching it.

Its promise is simple: know what changed, know why it matters, and ignore the
rest. Oreum optimises for attention efficiency, not engagement. A quiet day is
a successful result.

The problem is concrete. A single holding can generate ten or fifteen alert
emails a day from brokers and news services, and none of them get read —
there is no time or energy to follow every little thing. Oreum inverts that
ratio: one or two emails a day, sent only when something crosses an attention
threshold, with the platform behind them for investigation.

The differentiator is second-order intelligence over a small, densely
connected universe. Direct news about a holding the investor will see
anywhere; the connection they would have missed — a customer's capex is a
supplier's revenue — is the product. The most important information may not
mention the user's ticker.

## Who it is for

Oreum is for an investor with a watchlist or portfolio who wants a concise,
evidence-backed view of meaningful market situations. It is not for executing
trades, selecting stocks, or replacing investment advice.

## Boundaries

Oreum is not:

- a trading platform
- a stock picker or investment adviser
- a generic news aggregator
- an AI commentary or summarisation feed
- an influencer platform
- an engagement-maximising system

It does not forecast prices or tell users what to buy or sell.

## Product surfaces

### Email — “Do I need to care?”

Email is the primary surface and the fast daily filter. It should take
roughly one or two minutes to understand and may correctly say: “Nothing
significant requires your attention today.” A quiet email is only credible
when it shows its work, so the filtered-out counter — what was checked and
suppressed — is permanent content, not decoration. The email is what drives
every visit to the platform.

### Feed — “What happened?”

The Feed presents attention-ranked Intelligence Items. It should support a view
of the user's stocks and a broader-market view. Each item should communicate
what happened, direction, attention, context, explanation, and evidence.

### Ticker — “Why is it happening?”

The Ticker is the investigation surface. It should eventually combine current
price, historical trajectory, volume, technical context, important events,
market/sector context, exposures, connections, explanation, and evidence.

Technical indicators are context. RSI 72 does not mean “sell.”

## Experience principles

- Facts should be distinguishable from inference.
- Importance and personal relevance should be distinguishable.
- Direction should not be confused with attention.
- Evidence should be close to the claim it supports.
- The interface should make quiet days feel complete, not empty or broken.
- Current market state and finalized historical data should show their different
  timestamps and freshness.
